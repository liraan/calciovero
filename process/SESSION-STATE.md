**Model of the player (new engine, draft, 2026-11-05):** `L0-match/specs/11-player-characteristics.md` (L0.11, 1101 lines). **The model in one line:** all characteristics are declared in L0; the **formula** declares which ones an action uses; the **calculator** applies it; the **orchestrator** (`resolve_*`) decides when. Three components, three responsibilities, three APIs: the **custodian** (`pcfm-hidden`) holds the hidden values and answers one characteristic at a time; the **calculator** (new crate) applies a formula from `L0-match/balance/*.json` and asks the custodian only for the terms the formula declares; the **orchestrator** (`resolve_*`) chooses the formula and the situation and returns the outcome to the engine. The engine sees neither the hidden values nor the formulas. The boundary is compile-time: a mock custodian in the test verifies the calculator asks exactly the formula's terms, no more. A formula with an unknown term fails at load time, not mid-match. Four families — physical, technical, psychological, cognitive — plus three kinds of datum: **characteristic** (stable trait, saved with the identity), **behaviour** (scanning, positioning: never saved, recomputed), **state** (fatigue, confidence: per match or reconstructed). **Dimensions vs performances (§9):** height and weight are measured, visible properties of the body (`Player`); strength, passing, reading are performances, hidden (`pcfm-hidden`). **Prerequisite, not option:** OI-53 (keyed latents, `hash(world_seed, match_id, player_id, purpose)`). Until it is closed, every added characteristic shifts the random sequence of every player generated after the first. **Open:** ADR-028 must be rewritten (dimension vs performance, three components); `10-ground-truth.md` must be rewritten (the prototype `resolve_*` contract); the decisions in §12 are resolved as a model; what remains is calibration (§12.3), which belongs to L3 and blocks nothing.
